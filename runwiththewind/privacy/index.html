Privacy Policy: Run with the Wind

Last updated: November 28, 2026

Run with the Wind ("the app") is a personal project by Aditya Kumar Verma. It records workouts on a Wear OS watch, syncs them to an Android phone, and can upload them to Strava. Contact: runwiththewind.app [at] gmail.com.

What the app handles

  • Workout data recorded on your watch: GPS route, heart rate, distance, speed, elevation, timestamps and sport type.
  • If you connect Strava: the access and refresh tokens Strava issues, and the upload status of each activity.

Where your data goes

  • Workout data is stored on your watch and phone and transferred between them using the Wearable Data Layer provided by Google Play services. I run no servers and cannot see your data.
  • Only when you choose to upload, the app sends that activity to Strava, which handles it under its own Privacy Policy.
  • The app has no ads, analytics or tracking, and your data is never sold or shared with anyone else.

Permissions

Location (record your route and pace), body sensors / heart rate (record heart rate), physical activity (workout tracking), notifications (ongoing workout notification).

Strava

The app asks only for permission to upload activities for you (activity:write). It does not read your Strava profile, activities or other users' data. Tokens are stored encrypted on your phone and are never logged.

Retention and deletion

  • The watch removes track files automatically after a retention period of at most 30 days. The phone keeps activities until you delete them in the app.
  • "Disconnect Strava" in the app deletes the stored tokens and revokes access. You can also revoke access in Strava under Settings > My Apps.
  • Uninstalling removes all local data. Activities already uploaded to Strava stay there until you delete them on Strava.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Changes

I'll update this page and its date when the app's behavior changes.
