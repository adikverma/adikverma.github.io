How it works

Three steps from lacing up to reviewing your run.

  1. 01

    Record on the watch

    Start your run on Wear OS and track distance, pace, heart rate, elevation, and splits—without carrying your phone.

  2. 02

    Sync to the phone

    Once you finish, your watch sends the workout over to your Android phone so you can browse your route and charts.

  3. 03

    Upload to Strava

    Tap upload on any activity in the phone app to publish your run to Strava on your terms.

Under the hood

A quick look at the Android and Wear OS building blocks behind the app.

  • Kotlin

    Shared cleanly across the watch and phone apps.

  • Jetpack Compose

    Responsive layouts tuned for round watch faces and phones.

  • Health Services

    Battery-aware Wear OS sensor tracking for GPS, pace, and heart rate.

  • Room

    On-device local storage so your runs stay private with no server.

  • Wearable Data Layer

    Direct watch-to-phone sync with delivery acknowledgements.

  • WorkManager

    Reliable background sync and upload retries when connectivity returns.

Questions

Do I need Strava?

No. Recording runs on your watch and reviewing your history, routes, and charts on your phone work without any account. Strava is only needed if you choose to upload an activity.

Which devices are supported?

A Wear OS 4+ watch paired with an Android 13+ phone.

Is it on the Play Store?

Not yet. Run with the Wind is currently in active development.

Is it free?

Yes. It is a free personal project with no subscriptions, ads, or trackers.

Where does my data go?

Your workout data stays on your watch and phone. It is only sent to Strava when you tap upload on an activity. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Is it affiliated with Strava?

No. Run with the Wind is an independent personal project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Strava.
